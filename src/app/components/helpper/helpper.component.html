🔄 Turn Structure

A turn in Magic: The Gathering is divided into phases that define what you can do. The app doesn't enforce the order but helps you follow it visually and functionally:

  1. Beginning: Untap your permanents, resolve upkeep triggers, and draw a card.
  2. Main Phase 1: You may play a land (if you haven't already), cast creatures, enchantments, sorceries, etc.
  3. Combat Phase: Declare attackers, then the opponent blocks. Damage is assigned and resolved.
  4. Main Phase 2: Same as the first one, useful for casting spells after combat.
  5. End Step: End-of-turn effects trigger, and you discard if you have more than 7 cards in hand.

💡 The app lets you perform actions at any time to suit casual or physical play.

📦 Game Zones
  • Library: Your deck. You draw from here at the beginning of your turn or via effects. You can also search from here.
  • Hand: Hidden cards. You may play them if the cost and timing are right.
  • Battlefield: Where cards in play go. The app organizes them by type (creatures, lands, etc.).
  • Graveyard: Used, destroyed, or discarded cards. Some effects interact with this zone.
  • Exile: Temporarily or permanently out of the game, though some effects can bring them back.
  • Stack: Represents pending spells and abilities. The app displays it clearly in order.

💡 All zones are automatically synced and updated. You can move cards between them based on card effects.

🧩 Card and Permanent Types

Cards can be either spells or permanents. Some examples:

  • Land: One per turn. Produces mana to pay for other spells.
  • Creature: Attacks, blocks, and may have activated or triggered abilities.
  • Enchantment / Artifact: Continuous effects on the battlefield. Sometimes have activatable abilities.
  • Planeswalker: Enter with loyalty counters. You can use one ability per turn.
  • Sorcery: Can only be cast in your main phase with an empty stack.
  • Instant: Can be cast at any time, even during your opponent’s turn.

🌀 The Stack

The stack is where spells and abilities go before resolving:

  • Resolves in LIFO order (last in, first out).
  • Players may respond with instants or abilities before the top of the stack resolves.

💡 The app visually shows the stack's resolution order and allows step-by-step resolution.

⚡ Activating Abilities

Click on a card on the battlefield to access its activated abilities:

  • If it’s a mana ability, it executes directly.
  • If it’s a regular activated ability, it goes on the stack and you may respond before resolution.

🔍 The app automatically identifies which abilities are available and when they can be used.

🎯 Triggers

Triggers or triggered abilities are detected automatically based on events:

  • “When this enters the battlefield” → On enter (ETB)
  • “Whenever you cast a spell” → On spell cast
  • “When it dies” → When a creature dies (goes to graveyard from the battlefield)
  • “When it leaves the battlefield” → When a permanent leaves the field

💡 If multiple triggers happen at once, you can choose the resolution order.

🧭 In-App Help
  • Move cards between zones with actions like draw, exile, search, or discard.
  • Activate abilities directly from cards on the battlefield.
  • View and resolve the stack in a clear and controlled way.
  • Save and restore the full game state (deck, zones, stack, permanents, and log).
  • Use the views to easily check your deck, graveyard, exile, and battlefield.
  • The app automatically detects and displays triggers and abilities, helping track the game.
